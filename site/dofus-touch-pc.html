Play DOFUS Touch on PC

DOFUS Touch is played on phones and tablets. To play it on a computer, with a big screen, a mouse and a keyboard, there are four ways, and they are not equal.

Emulators: BlueStacks, LDPlayer and the rest

An emulator runs Android on the PC, and DOFUS Touch with it. It is the best known way, and the one to avoid: Ankama lists emulators and modified clients among the practices forbidden on DOFUS Touch, on its support site. An account caught using one risks a sanction.

The phone plugged into a screen

With a USB-C to HDMI adapter, some phones show their screen on a monitor, and a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard do the rest. The game stays the official app and no software is needed. But not every phone sends video out of its USB-C port, and you need one screen per phone.

Mirroring: the phone's screen in a window on the PC

The game stays on the phone: a program copies its screen into a window on the PC and sends the mouse and keyboard back. scrcpy, free and open source, is the reference. It is used from the command line, though, and knows nothing about the game: one window per phone, to set up and place yourself.

Beware of modified mirroring tools passed around: a program that drives your phone must come from a source you trust.

DT Hub: mirroring built for DOFUS Touch

DT Hub is built on scrcpy, without the command line. It finds every account on every phone, main profile and cloned profile, and opens each in its own window: as tabs, side by side or in a grid, with F1 to F8 to switch between them. The game is not modified, and DT Hub never plays for you. It is free, open source, for Windows.

What about several accounts?

Multi-accounting is allowed on DOFUS Touch, within limits: never more than two accounts in some fights, such as the Kolossium, and every action made by hand, without automation. The exact rules are on Ankama's support site. We do not speak for Ankama: when in doubt, its support has the last word.

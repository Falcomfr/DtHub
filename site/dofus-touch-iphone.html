Play DOFUS Touch on PC with an iPhone

To play from an iPhone or iPad on a big screen, with mouse and keyboard, its screen has to be mirrored to the computer. Until now, that meant paid software.

What there was

scrcpy, the free reference for mirroring, only knows Android. For an iPhone, guides point to Vysor, which is paid. Emulators run the Android version of the game on the PC, and Ankama lists them among forbidden practices, on its support site.

DT Hub: free, on Windows

Since iOS 27, an iPhone in Developer Mode can send its screen to a computer and take taps from it. DT Hub uses that: the iPhone's or iPad's screen opens in a window on the PC, the mouse and keyboard work in it, and the game stays the official App Store app.

  • One account per device: iOS cannot clone an app
  • In the same list, with the same shortcuts and tabs as the Android accounts: an iPhone and an Android phone play side by side
  • The iPhone's screen dims instead of switching off
  • Free and open source, no bot, no macro

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC, 64-bit
  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 27 or later
  • Developer Mode turned on, on the device: Settings › Privacy & Security
  • The Apple Devices app from the Microsoft Store, on the PC
  • The device plugged in over USB, unlocked, and trusted
  • A PC that decodes HEVC video: a recent graphics card, or the HEVC Video Extensions from the Microsoft Store

In DT Hub, the "I have an iPhone or an iPad" link in the add-phone window guides the rest. The install guide covers every step.

And on a Mac?

Not with an iPhone yet. On a Mac, DT Hub shows Android phones: play DOFUS Touch on Mac.

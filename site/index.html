From your own phones. Not an emulator: the game runs on your real phone, DT Hub mirrors it onto the PC and arranges the windows.

What it does not do, and never will

This is the most important thing on this page, and it comes before the feature list.

  • No bot, no macro, no input replay. Nothing ever plays for you, at any point.
  • No screen recognition for playing. The app does not read the game, it displays it.
  • No way to repeat one action across several accounts. A click goes to one window. That is a design refusal, not a missing feature: proposals in that direction are closed without discussion.
  • No game asset embedded, no modification of the game. The phone is never modified, and scrcpy is used without a single change.
  • No antivirus disabled, no exclusion, no administrator rights. An app that needs your antivirus disarmed is not an app you should trust.
  • Nothing is ever sent on its own. Incident reports are composed for you, stripped of anything identifying, and you decide whether to send them.

What it does

You install DOFUS Touch on the phone's main profile, then again on a cloned profile such as Xiaomi's Second Space or Dual Apps. Each profile holds its own account, and DT Hub finds every one of them.

  • One file to run. No installer, no administrator rights, no .NET to install. Everything it keeps sits in one folder you can simply delete.
  • Stacked windows. Every account opens at the same place and size, so they overlay exactly.
  • Or one window with tabs. Any account docks into a single frame, is picked from a tab bar, dragged into another order, or pulled back out.
  • Any Android profile id. A cloned profile is not always 999, and DT Hub never assumes it is.
  • USB or Wi-Fi. Over the air it finds the phone on the network and reconnects on its own at every launch, even when the port changed after a reboot.
  • Placement and sizes from the keyboard. Four absolute sizes, full screen, tiling, and every window back to its place.
  • Clipboard both ways. Through scrcpy's own mechanism, into a docked tab as readily as a free window.
  • A quest guide. 782 quests, 83 dungeons, 2 raids and 8 lairs, sorted by level and area, remembering where you stopped.
  • An almanac. The day's offering and bonus, without leaving the PC.
  • English, French and Spanish. Following your Windows display language.
  • It tells you what is wrong. Low battery, phone heating up, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, game missing from a profile, pairing to redo: each one is named under the device it concerns.

Built with player feedback

Not a slogan. Here is what players' remarks changed, and what was measured afterwards.

  • "The game keeps running when I close the window." The phone kept changing address and the stop order went nowhere. Fixed, and an empty card was also left behind in the phone's app list: it is no longer created.
  • "The app takes a while to start." It rediscovered every profile before checking whether there was anything to open. The window now appears in 840 ms instead of 3,950.
  • "I have to click twice to forget a device." One click is enough, and it no longer claims in between that the game is not installed.
  • "Indexing the guide takes time." It reported only one of its five stages. All five are now named, yesterday's list stays on screen, and the whole thing dropped under fifteen seconds.
  • "I cannot click inside the window." Some manufacturer layers refuse input injection despite the Android permission. The app detects it, says so, and offers the one workaround that does work.

Everything is written down in the changelog, and the reasoning behind each decision in docs/DECISIONS.md, in French. Report what bothers you: that is how the list above got written.

Check what you downloaded

The file is not signed: that checksum is the only way to know you have the binary this repository built. One line in PowerShell, from the folder where both files sit. 

(Get-FileHash DtHub.exe -Algorithm SHA256).Hash -eq (Get-Content DtHub.exe.sha256).Split(' ')[0]

True means it is the right file. The app then checks on its own whether a newer version exists, and never installs anything without asking.

Windows will warn you the first time

SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" on any downloaded program it does not recognise. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Why it is unsigned and what it would take to change that is written in docs/CONFIANCE.md, in French. Better said before the blue screen shows up.

What you need

  • Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64 bit.
  • An Android 11 phone or later. USB reaches further back than wireless.
  • A cloned Android profile already created on the phone. DT Hub does not create one.
  • An internet connection on first launch, once. 19 MB of third party tools from Google and Genymobile, each checked against a known fingerprint before use.
  • Microsoft WebView2 for the guide windows. Ships with Windows 11, reaches Windows 10 through Edge. Without it, everything else still works.

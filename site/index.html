Several DOFUS Touch accounts side by side on Windows

From your own phones. Not an emulator: the game runs on your phone, DT Hub mirrors it and arranges the windows.

  • FreeNo ads, no account to create, no paid feature.
  • Within the game's rulesNo bot, no macro, no modification of the game. One gesture, one action, one account.
  • Guides with permissionThose of papycha.fr, with their agreement.
  • Built with youEvery reported defect gets fixed.
Download DtHub.exe Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit · nothing to install · MIT licence

The windows

  • One account per Android profile, all found on their own
  • Every window at the same size and the same place
  • Or all of them in a tabbed frame, reordered by dragging
  • Four sizes from the keyboard, plus full screen
  • Tiling, and every window back to its place
  • A window can be locked, and the placements leave it alone

The panel

  • Shown or hidden with Ctrl+P
  • Your phones and the accounts each one hosts
  • The Android profile behind every account
  • The phone's battery level
  • Open or close an account in one click
  • It says what is wrong: low battery, phone heating up, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, pairing to redo

The guides

  • The guides from papycha.fr, beside the game, with their agreement
  • Ctrl+Q shows or hides them
  • 782 quests, 83 dungeons, 2 raids, 8 lairs
  • Sorted by level, size and area, coordinates included
  • A quest step by step, and where you stopped

The Almanax

  • The day's offering and who to bring it to
  • The bonus, written out in full
  • The quest and meryde that go with it
  • The week at a glance, and any date from the calendar
  • Read from the official Almanax, filtered down to what concerns DOFUS Touch
  • The window does not show the page: it reads it and draws itself

The rest

  • One file to run. No installer, no administrator rights, no .NET to install.
  • USB or Wi-Fi. Over the air it finds the phone again at every launch, even when the port changed.
  • Clipboard both ways.
  • English, French and Spanish. Following your Windows language.
  • An incident report ready to paste. Stripped of your serial numbers, addresses and account names. Nothing is sent unless you decide to.

Within the game's rules

The game runs on your phone, exactly as Ankama publishes it. DT Hub shows its screen and forwards your gestures. Nothing more, and that is deliberate.

  • No bot, no macro, no input replay. Nothing ever plays for you, at any point.
  • No action repeated across several accounts. A click goes to one window. That is a design refusal: proposals in that direction are closed without discussion.
  • The game is not modified, nor is the phone. The installed app stays Ankama's own, untouched.
  • No reading of the game's memory, no network interception. DT Hub only sees the picture the phone sends it.
  • No game asset embedded. No image, no icon, no sound. The only images in the project are drawn for it.

Download

  • An Android 11 phone or later, with a cloned profile already created.

Play DOFUS Touch on PC, several accounts at once

From your own phones. Not an emulator. Written by a DOFUS Touch player, for players.

  • FreeNo ads, no account, no paid feature.
  • Within the game's rulesNo bot, no macro, no modification of the game.
  • Guides with permissionThose of papycha.fr, with their agreement.
  • Built with youEvery reported defect gets fixed.
Download DtHub.exe Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit · nothing to install · MIT licence

Built for DOFUS Touch

  • Windows at the size the game requires
  • Display cut for the game
  • Found on every Android profile, whatever its id
  • Closing a window really disconnects the character
  • Guides and Almanax filtered on Touch

The windows

  • One account per Android profile
  • All at the same size, the same place
  • Or all of them in a tabbed frame
  • Four sizes from the keyboard, and full screen
  • Tiling in one shortcut
  • A window can be locked

The panel

  • Ctrl+P shows or hides it
  • Your phones and their accounts
  • The Android profile behind each one
  • The battery level
  • Open or close an account in one click
  • Battery, heat, slow Wi-Fi: it warns

The guides

  • The guides from papycha.fr, beside the game
  • Ctrl+Q shows or hides them
  • 782 quests, 83 dungeons, 2 raids, 8 lairs
  • Sorted by level, size and area
  • The steps, and where you were

The Almanax

  • The day's offering and who to bring it to
  • The bonus, written out in full
  • The quest and the meryde
  • The week at a glance
  • Any date from the calendar

Within the game's rules

  • No bot, no macro, no input replay
  • No action repeated across several accounts
  • The game is not modified, nor is the phone
  • No reading of memory, no network interception
  • No game asset embedded

The rest

  • One file, no installer
  • USB or Wi-Fi, with automatic reconnection
  • Clipboard both ways
  • English, French and Spanish
  • An anonymised incident report, ready to paste

Download

Download DtHub.exe SHA256 checksum · all releases
  • Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit
  • Android 11 or later, with a cloned profile
  • An internet connection on first launch, for 19 MB of tools
  • Windows warns on first run, the file not being signed. Why, in French

DT Hub

Several DOFUS Touch accounts side by side on Windows, from your own phones.

Not an emulator. The game runs on your real phone; DT Hub mirrors it onto the PC and arranges the windows. Built on ADB and scrcpy.

Download DtHub.exe Windows 10 1809 or later, 64 bit · nothing to install · MIT licence

What DT Hub does

You install DOFUS Touch on the phone's main profile, then again on a cloned profile such as Xiaomi's Second Space or Dual Apps. Each profile holds its own account. DT Hub finds every one of them and opens each in its own window.

One file to run

No installer, no administrator rights, no .NET to install. Everything it keeps sits in one folder; delete it and the app is back to new.

Stacked windows, or tabs

Every account opens at the same place and size so they overlay exactly, or docks into a single frame with a tab bar you can reorder by dragging.

Any Android profile id

A cloned profile is not always 999, and DT Hub never assumes it is. It reads what the phone declares.

USB or Wi-Fi

Over the air it finds the phone on the network, fills in its address, and reconnects on its own at every launch, even when the port changed after a reboot.

A quest guide

The guides from papycha.fr beside the game, remembering where you stopped. They are the site's own pages, shown as they are.

Three languages

English, French and Spanish, following your Windows display language, and English for anything else.

In pictures

Two accounts side by side

Each account gets its own window, at the same size and place, so they overlay exactly. Ctrl+Tab cycles through them, and every window title carries a reminder of the shortcuts.

Or one window with tabs

Any account can be docked into a single frame and picked from a tab bar, dragged into another order, or pulled back out. Typing and the clipboard reach the docked game exactly as they reach a free window, and the frame answers to the size and placement shortcuts like any other game window.

The configurator

Shown or hidden with Ctrl+P. It holds the device list, the accounts each one hosts with their Android profile, the phone's battery level, and the shortcuts. Every account opens, closes and locks in place from its own row.

Guides, beside the game

Ctrl+Q opens the guide window. It follows a quest step by step, remembers where you were, and says at the foot of the page what the quest unlocks. It never plays for you: it shows the site's pages.

And enough to find your way

782 quests, 83 dungeons, 2 raids and 8 lairs, sorted by level, size and area, with their coordinates and their prerequisites. That list is one DT Hub builds itself.

Shortcuts

They only apply while a DT Hub window is focused, so Ctrl+Tab keeps working normally in your browser. All of them can be changed in the configurator.

ShortcutAction
Ctrl+PShow or hide the configurator
Ctrl+TabNext account, or next tab inside the frame
Ctrl+RPut every window back in place
Ctrl+TTile the windows side by side
Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+4Four window sizes
Ctrl+5Full screen
Ctrl+QShow or hide the guides
Ctrl+0Close every window

What it deliberately does not do

DT Hub displays, launches, arranges and forwards your own input. It contains no bot, no macro, no input replay, no screen recognition for playing, and no way to mirror one action across several accounts. One gesture is one action, on one account.

It never disables your antivirus, never adds exclusions and never asks for administrator rights. Shortcuts use RegisterHotKey, not a keyboard hook: only the combinations you configured are ever intercepted.

Download

Download DtHub.exe and its checksum, DtHub.exe.sha256

Check what you downloaded

The file is not signed, so that checksum is the only way to know you have the binary this repository built. One line in PowerShell, from the folder where both files sit: 

(Get-FileHash DtHub.exe -Algorithm SHA256).Hash -eq (Get-Content DtHub.exe.sha256).Split(' ')[0]

True means it is the right file.

Windows will warn you the first time

SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" on any downloaded program it does not recognise, and an unsigned one never earns that recognition. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Why it is unsigned, what it would take to change that, and what signing actually buys are all written down in docs/CONFIANCE.md, in French. We would rather explain it before the blue screen shows up.

What you need

  • Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64 bit.
  • An Android 11 phone or later for virtual displays and wireless pairing. USB reaches further back.
  • A cloned Android profile already created on the phone. DT Hub does not create one.
  • An internet connection on first launch, once: 19 MB of third party tools from Google and Genymobile, each checked against a known fingerprint before use.
  • Microsoft WebView2 for the guide windows. It ships with Windows 11 and reaches Windows 10 through Edge. Without it, DT Hub says so and everything else still works.

Thanks

The guides DT Hub shows are those of papycha.fr, written and kept up to date by one person. Nothing the guide window displays would exist without that work, and the permission to display it was given freely. Go and have a look: that is where everything is written.
